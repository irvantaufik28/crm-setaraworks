# SetaraWorks CRM Design Direction

Reading this as: internal sales CRM for the SetaraWorks admin, in a compact operational style, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Identity

- Personality: precise, dependable, direct, and calm under a high information load.
- Palette: SetaraWorks navy anchors navigation and priority data. Brand blue marks the single primary action. Paper grey separates working areas without decorative effects.
- Typography: Arial and the system sans stack keep dense tables and forms fast to render and easy to scan. Tight heading spacing carries the sharper SetaraWorks brand voice.
- Motif: numbered navigation and section indexes make the CRM feel like an operational workbench and create consistent orientation.

## Decision reasons

- Color: navy establishes the SetaraWorks identity, while blue is reserved for primary actions and key data bars.
- Layout: the dashboard puts follow-up priority before summary metrics because daily action is the product's main job.
- Typography: a familiar sans serif reduces reading friction in dense tables, forms, and pipeline cards.
- Spacing: compact controls support frequent admin work, while larger gaps only separate major workflow groups.
- Cards: cards appear only for movable pipeline records and bounded panels; tabular records remain tables or lists.
- Motion: only navigation and drag feedback move, because motion should confirm state rather than decorate the workspace.
- Theme: the fixed light workspace supports daytime, information-heavy sales work and keeps printed or exported screens predictable.
- Mobile: the sidebar becomes a labelled menu, forms stack, summary bands reflow, and wide operational tables use contained horizontal scrolling.
